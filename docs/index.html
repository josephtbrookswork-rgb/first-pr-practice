Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Solaris doesn't have accounts, logins, or sign-in of any kind. Everything works right away.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. Your routine, pantry, check-ins, and history are never sent to a server — Solaris has no backend and no cloud sync.

How do reminders work?

You can set a reminder time for each of your Dawn, Midday, and Dusk phases. Solaris uses standard iOS notifications, so reminders work even when the app isn't open — just make sure notification permission is allowed.

Why does Solaris ask for my location?

Location is used only to personalize the sun exposure estimate on your dashboard. It's optional — you can use every other part of the app without granting it — and it never leaves your device.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Since nothing is backed up externally, deleting Solaris removes your routine, pantry, and history along with it. There's currently no export or restore feature.
