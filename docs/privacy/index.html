Privacy Policy

Last updated August 7, 2026

The short version

Solaris doesn't have accounts, doesn't have a server, and doesn't share your data with anyone. Everything you enter — your routine, pantry, check-ins, and history — stays stored locally on your device. The only outside data Solaris touches is your device's location, and only if you choose to share it, only to personalize an on-screen estimate, and it's never transmitted anywhere.

What Solaris collects

Location. If you grant location permission, Solaris uses your approximate coordinates to personalize the sun exposure estimate shown on your dashboard. This is optional — every other feature works without it — and the value is used only on your device, in the moment, to compute that estimate. It is not stored on any server, not linked to your identity, and not used for advertising or tracking of any kind.

Everything else you enter — your name, age, vitamin schedule, pantry items, daily check-ins, and history — is entered voluntarily by you and stored only in the app's local storage on your device.

What Solaris does not do

  • No account creation, sign-in, or user identifiers of any kind
  • No servers or cloud sync — Solaris has no backend to send data to
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs
  • No sharing or selling of data to third parties, because none is collected off-device to begin with
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives only on your device for as long as the app is installed. Deleting Solaris deletes all of it — there is currently no export or cloud backup, so nothing persists anywhere else.

Your choices

You can grant or revoke location access at any time in your device's Settings app, and enable or disable reminder notifications from within Solaris itself. Neither choice affects the rest of the app's functionality.

Children's privacy

Solaris does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and since the app collects no data off-device in the first place, there is nothing to gather regardless of a user's age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it. Since Solaris has no way to contact users directly (no accounts, no push beyond local reminders), please check back here if you have concerns.
